Findings & recommendations

  • Parameters were tuned on the older data. Treat in-sample numbers as optimistic; the out-of-sample row is the more honest guide but is still a small sample.
  • A win rate around a third means long losing streaks are normal (see 95th pct streak above). Size positions so a streak that long is survivable.
  • Trade gold only, for now. The same rules do not show a reliable edge on FX, indices or crypto (table above). Forex pairs and indices only have ~2 years of free hourly data, so those verdicts are tentative; crypto has 9 years and still fails.
  • Stop and target are checked on H4 bars; when both fall in one bar the stop wins (conservative) — keep that.
  • Next experiments: walk-forward re-optimisation, and a parameter-sensitivity grid to check the edge is not one lucky setting.

Research output, not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
