Be realistic

  • No signal is “sure”. Historically about 36% of A-grade trades won; the edge comes from winners paying several times the loser.
  • A losing streak of 15 trades in a row is within normal bounds (95th percentile); the worst drawdown to plan for is about 22R. At 0.5% risk per trade that is roughly 11% of the account.
  • Resampling the history, about 3% of paths ended with a net loss. Newer data (2024–26) was better than older data, but it is only 24 trades.
  • The rules were tuned on 2013–2021 and confirmed on 2024–2026; they have never traded real money. Start small or on a demo account first.
